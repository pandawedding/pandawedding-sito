Alina and Omrane live in Dubai, but for their wedding they chose the hills outside Verona. It was an elegant day, at times almost royal, where every detail was meant to be looked at: the flowers, the gowns, the light, the frescoed halls. The kind of wedding where photography and film stop simply recording and become an aesthetic pursuit.

We told their story side by side with the wedding planner who had been with them from the very start: shared timings, ideas passed back and forth, one creative direction. A destination wedding in Verona comes together through teamwork, and through the trust of a couple who knew exactly what they wanted.

## Morning at Borgo 27

The getting ready took place at Borgo 27 in Grezzana, just a few minutes from Villa Arvedi. We started from above, with the drone, to set the scene right away: the hills, the green, a day about to begin.

## Villa Arvedi and the Hall of the Titans

Then on to Villa Arvedi, a historic villa in the Valpantena hills, surrounded by cypresses, vineyards and an Italian garden of boxwood hedges shaped like a maze. We work here often and are always happy to come back: to us, it's one of the best wedding venues in Verona.

![Drone view of Villa Arvedi and its Italian garden among the Valpantena vineyards](../../../assets/real-weddings/alina-omrane/13-drone-view-villa-arvedi-italian-garden.jpg)

Before the ceremony we took Alina into the Hall of the Titans (Sala dei Titani), already set for dinner: monumental frescoes, crystal chandeliers, burgundy velvet tablecloths and gilded chairs. For a wedding photographer at Villa Arvedi, no room tells the regal character of the day better.

![Alina in the Hall of the Titans at Villa Arvedi, among the tables set for dinner](../../../assets/real-weddings/alina-omrane/14-bride-hall-of-titans-villa-arvedi.jpg)

![Alina from behind, veil and train beneath the chandelier of the Hall of the Titans](../../../assets/real-weddings/alina-omrane/15-bride-veil-train-chandelier.jpg)

Dinner was served in the Hall of the Titans. In the warm evening light, the frescoes and chandeliers took on a new depth. The table settings, the menus and the flowers were the wedding planner's work, and they deserved to be captured with the same care that went into creating them.

After dinner the couple stepped out onto the balustrade for the fireworks. It's one of the hardest and most beautiful moments to photograph: darkness, light that changes with every burst, and two people who always have to stay at the center.
