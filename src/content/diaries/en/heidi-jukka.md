Heidi and Jukka came from Finland with a clear idea: to live the Italian dolce vita, unhurried, surrounded by the people they love. For their wedding in Verona they chose Villa La Zambonina, a historic villa that so many couples from abroad pick for their destination wedding, and they made the most of it from start to finish: getting ready, ceremony, dinner and party, all in one place.

It was September 29th, a cool, bright day, with that soft early-autumn light that makes everything look its best. A luxury wedding made of carefully chosen details, true friends and a real desire to celebrate.

## Morning at the villa

The shoes, the gown hanging in the blue room, the makeup, the glasses ready to be filled: the getting ready took place in the villa's own rooms, among painted wardrobes, old portraits and terracotta floors.

## The day after, by the pool

The party didn't end there. The next day Heidi and Jukka wanted a brunch by the pool, and we captured that too, in photos and film: friends still together, no schedule and plenty of fun.

![Drone view of the pool at Villa La Zambonina during the brunch, with loungers and the pergola](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/49-drone-view-pool-brunch-villa-la-zambonina.jpg)

White heart-shaped floats in the pool, powder-pink flowers on the tables, an instant camera and a guestbook waiting to be filled with polaroids.

![White heart-shaped floats in the pool](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/50-heart-pool-floats-brunch.jpg)
![Polaroids and an instant camera on the red guestbook table](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/52-instant-photos-guestbook-brunch.jpg)

At the bar, spritz and signature cocktails; at the stove, eggs cooked to order in a copper pan. Then gelato, croissants, cheeses, and the saxophone playing again, this time by the water.

![Eggs, bacon and spinach cooked to order in a copper pan](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/54-eggs-cooked-copper-pan-brunch.jpg)
![Friends chatting on the red loungers by the pool](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/60-friends-red-loungers-brunch.jpg)

![The bride in a short white dress and sunglasses under the pergola](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/56-bride-white-dress-brunch.jpg)
![The bride raising her spritz for a toast on the lawn](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/57-bride-spritz-toast-brunch.jpg)

And at the end, all together once more: the bride's friends by the pool for one last toast, then out of the loggia one by one, spritz in hand. The dolce vita, right to the very last day.

![The bride and her friends toasting all together by the pool](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/62-bride-friends-toast-by-the-pool.jpg)

![The bride coming down the loggia steps with her spritz](../../../assets/real-weddings/heidi-jukka/64-bride-spritz-loggia-steps.jpg)

To us, that's what a destination wedding in Verona is about: not just one day, but an experience to live and to relive. In the photos and in the wedding film, because a wedding video in Verona should also capture this, the slow, happy rhythm of people who took the time to enjoy it.
