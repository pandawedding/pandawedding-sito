Some couples need a little time in front of the camera. Not Donya and Kueni: they were already laughing. Full of energy and enthusiasm, smiling from the first minute to the last dance, they gave us one of those days when the best thing to do is stay close, pay attention and not interrupt a thing.

On September 25th, at Villa Arvedi, two cultures and two families came together: a Persian ceremony in the afternoon, traditional Nigerian dress in the evening. In between, petals, embraces and a happiness that needed no direction.

## Kueni and his friends

The shoes, the watch, the cufflinks: everything was laid out in perfect order. Then his friends arrived, and the order didn't last long. Three pairs of hands for one bow tie and one cummerbund, with Kueni in the middle, laughing.

## A Persian ceremony in Italy

The ceremony took place in the courtyard of Villa Arvedi, at the foot of the staircase of its Baroque chapel, among the cypresses and vineyards of Valpantena.

![The courtyard of Villa Arvedi with its Baroque chapel and the guests before the ceremony](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/16-ceremony-courtyard-villa-arvedi-chapel.jpg)

In front of them, the *sofreh aghd*, the spread at the heart of every Persian wedding: the mirror and candlesticks, the rock sugar, the pomegranates, the wheat, the rosebuds. Every object is a wish, and when you photograph and film a Persian wedding in Italy, each one deserves its own moment.

![Sofreh aghd details: rings, sugar crystals and rosebuds](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/17-sofreh-aghd-details-rings-rosebuds.jpg)
![Hydrangeas and roses on the stone balustrade of the staircase](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/18-ceremony-flowers-stone-staircase.jpg)

Kueni was waiting among the flowers. Donya arrived smiling, and never stopped.

![The groom waiting for the bride in front of the flower-covered staircase](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/19-groom-waiting-ceremony-flowers.jpg)
![The bride being walked to the ceremony, in black and white](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/20-bride-walking-aisle-black-white.jpg)

![Drone view of the ceremony in the courtyard of Villa Arvedi, among cypresses and vineyards](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/21-drone-view-ceremony-villa-arvedi-vineyards.jpg)

Sitting side by side in front of the sofreh, they reached for each other's hands before they even looked up. Then the most awaited moment of the rite: a white cloth held above them, and sugar over the cloth, so that their life together may be sweet.

![Bride and groom sitting in front of the sofreh aghd during the Persian ceremony](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/22-bride-groom-sofreh-aghd-persian-ceremony.jpg)

![The white cloth held over the bride and groom in the Persian sugar ritual at Villa Arvedi](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/23-persian-ceremony-sugar-cloth-villa-arvedi.jpg)

When they exchanged rings, the emotion turned into laughter. The real, full kind, with a hand in the air to show the ring to everyone.

![Bride and groom laughing as they exchange rings, in black and white](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/24-bride-groom-laughing-ceremony-black-white.jpg)
![Bride and groom laughing and showing their rings to the guests](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/25-bride-groom-showing-wedding-rings.jpg)

## Cutting the cake in Nigerian dress

And then the best surprise of the evening. The doors of the villa opened and Donya and Kueni walked out to a different beat: velvet and gold embroidery, coral beads, an emerald headwrap, a top hat, a fan of red feathers. Traditional Nigerian dress, worn with all the pride and joy of people who are truly celebrating.

![Bride and groom in traditional Nigerian dress at the entrance of Villa Arvedi](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/42-bride-groom-nigerian-traditional-dress.jpg)

![Bride and groom in Nigerian dress with their arms raised, the bride holding a red feather fan](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/43-bride-groom-nigerian-dress-arms-raised.jpg)

Under a tree glowing with candles, the cake cutting brought everything together: the dress of Nigeria and the Persian knife dance, where the cake knife has to be earned, between dancing and laughter.

![The Persian knife dance next to the wedding cake](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/44-persian-knife-dance-wedding-cake.jpg)
![The bride in Nigerian dress during the knife dance](../../../assets/real-weddings/donya-kueni/45-bride-nigerian-dress-knife-dance.jpg)

The rest of the night belonged to the dance floor. If we had to pick a single image to tell this day, it would be hard: in almost all of them, the two of them are laughing. And that's exactly how it went.
